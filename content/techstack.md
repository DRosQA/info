### generative AI
#### working with AI
With my specific skillset, **I find it easier and faster to work without any genAI assistance**, and it ensures I can vouch for the reliability of the work I provide. Because of that, I do not work with genAI.

I enjoy learning and I've spent my entire life looking up information and compiling it into a personal reservoir of knowledge and source references. I am skilled in coming up with keywords and collecting information from available materials. As such, I have no need for genAI in the usual ways it is applied — as a natural-language interface to search engines or as text- and idea-generator.

#### generative AI in projects
I understand that not everyone has the same skillset as me, and that this "no-AI" approach is not feasible for everyone. **I don't have qualms with coworkers using generative AI** to supplement their coding or writing skills so long that it doesn't impact the quality nor their ownership of the results.

#### general doubts
I do not think genAI has any place in creative work. It is only capable of reproducing variations of what's already been made and included in the training data, and doesn't bring anything new to the table, which is the primary goal of creativity. I also **oppose to using image generation AI models in any way, shape or form.** They're trained on material that is primarily obtained illegally, against the creators' wishes and ignoring the licensing. 

Additionally, I find it necessary to acknowledge that these days, genAI is one of the biggest negative impacts on ecology, economy and society at large. I believe that using it at all has to come with reconciliation of, and ideally offsetting the consequences of utilizing such technology.
